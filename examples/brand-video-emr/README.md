# Worked example: a 45-second explainer (EMR record translation)

The `video.json` and `beats.mjs` that produced a real branded short. Copy them as a starting point, then change the
words. The graphics (a counting stat with a dot grid, a record moving between two systems and garbling, a years
counter, a two-system field comparison) each show what the speaker is saying instead of repeating the captions.
Build with `node scripts/build-video.mjs <project> --format 4x5 --sfx --music` (see docs/BUILDER.md).
The footage, transcripts and renders of that video are private and not in the repo.
